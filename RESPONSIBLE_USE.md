# Responsible use

SwarmSAR exists to help people. It coordinates drones to **find** humans so
that rescuers can reach them faster in disasters, wilderness, and maritime
emergencies, and to support inspection and mapping.

## Permitted uses

- Search-and-rescue and disaster response
- Locating and mapping people who need assistance
- Infrastructure inspection, environmental mapping, research and education

## Prohibited uses

By using this software you agree **not** to use it, in whole or in part, to:

- target, harm, or apply force to any person the system detects;
- integrate with, cue, or trigger any weapon or munition;
- build an autonomous system that selects or engages human targets;
- conduct surveillance that violates people's rights or applicable law.

The human-presence detector and the alert dispatcher are designed for one
purpose: handing a location to people who can help. There is no targeting or
engagement capability in this project, and adding one is out of scope and
against these terms.
